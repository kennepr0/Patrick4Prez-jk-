Balladhospital monopoly

He voted for the hospital monopoly. It's also on his income form. Small world!

Crowe voted for the bill that allowed the Ballad Health merger, which left much of our region with one hospital system. His own primary opponent pointed out that Crowe lists Ballad as a source of income. Crowe also serves as a vice president of Shared Health Services in Johnson City, while chairing the Senate committee that oversees health policy.

Sources: WCYB, July 2026 · Wikipedia
